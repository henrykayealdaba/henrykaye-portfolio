I did not have a special reason for becoming a programmer.
It just felt like a natural path for someone like me who loved spending time on the computer. I was curious about how things worked, how websites were built and how apps were made. That curiosity slowly turned into passion.
\
Before I knew it, I was writing my first line of code, and something clicked. It felt amazing to understand something that once seemed impossible. There's a special kind of joy in overcoming challenges you once thought were too hard to grasp.
\
"I did this? I can't believe I actually did it"
\
Yep, that's me.
