Someday, I want to go to Japan and visit real shrines (神社).
I'm drawn to their peaceful atmosphere and want to understand the deeper value of their culture.
\
I'll keep reading, learning, dreaming and imagining what would it feel to stand in that place,
to hear the wind pass through the trees, and to breath in the air filled with centuries of quiet devotion.
\
Even from afar, the thought brings me a sense of calm and purpose, reminding me why this goal matters so much to me.
\
Maybe I can't go right now, not today, not tomorrow, not even next month.
But someday, I'll achieve this. It's one of my goals, and I'll hold on to it.
